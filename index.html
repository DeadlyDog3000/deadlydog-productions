Forester: Reckoning

The Forester story lived in first person, in 3D. Hamburg, 1683: carry your father's ledger to the harbour on the last ordinary evening, run through the marsh gate when they come for him, and rebuild a burned cabin in the old woods. Hunt, hide from the lantern, keep the fire through the winter — then grow the clearing into a town, and the town, age by age, into a city. Then take your company into the great battles of the age, and a kingdom onto the campaign map. A story, a survival game, a city builder, a battle game and a grand strategy — all one game.

Standards in Smoke

A Napoleonic-era strategy game on a hex map. Take one of twenty-one historical nations, found cities, research your way out of the crossbow, and hold a court full of noble houses that are running out of patience with you. Seasons bite, formation decides battles, and an ignored house will raise a rebellion outside your own capital.

Forester

A pixel-art colony builder about family survival. Hamburg, 1683: expelled from the city for "Malicious Affairs" — your father executed, the rest of the family barely escaping — your family fled into the woodlands, far, far away. Rebuild what was lost: recruit wanderers, research two tech trees, raise walls against raiders, and carve your empire onto the map of Europe.
