Download everything How to install

Install in a minute

For Epic, GOG, or a Steam copy without the Workshop. On Steam you can simply subscribe on the Workshop instead.

Download

Pick a mod below, or take The Complete Collection, which holds all of them in one.

Unzip into Mods

Documents\My Games\OldWorld\Mods

Make the Mods folder if it isn't there. Each mod is a folder of its own inside it.

Switch it on

Start Old World, open Mods from the main menu, tick the mod, and start or load a game. Your saves keep working.

Using The Complete Collection? Leave the single mods off, since they're already inside it.

All Wonders

Every wonder you own, in every game.

What it does

Vanilla Old World quietly switches off every wonder above 13 when a new game starts. This mod lifts the cap, so every wonder from the base game and every DLC you own can be raced for, built, and lost to your rivals.

Armies & looks
No. XV

Armies

Twenty men to a legion. Every unit an army.

What it does

Every unit on the map is drawn with far more soldiers. A legion is a dense block of 20 men, warbands are loose mobs of 20, archers stand in staggered lines of 20, cavalry ride in squadrons of 12, and chariots, elephants and siege engines come in batteries. Settlers, scouts, caravans and disciples travel in small groups (workers stay single, because the game sends every worker figure to the same build spot). Figures are scaled down so every unit still fits its hex. Warbands and skirmishers stand a little out of line so they look like people, not a spreadsheet.

Armies & looks
No. XIX

Fallen Soldiers

Wounded units lose men. The rest break ranks.

What it does

When a unit takes damage, men in its formation fall and die. A legion at half health shows half its men; a unit at a quarter of its health is down to a quarter. The men drop when the clash is over, using the game's own death animation, lie where they fell for a moment, and fade away.

The survivors lose their neat ranks as their comrades fall. A fresh unit stands in parade-ground lines; one that has lost half its men stands loose and ragged, every man a little out of place and turned a little off the line; a unit down to its last few is a scattered knot of men. When the unit heals, men return and the ranks close up again.

No. XX

Loyal Families

The great houses forgive more and value their place at court.

What it does

Every grudge a family holds against you is half as strong: a slight at court, a refused law, a city given away, an exiled member. Grudges still last just as long, so a family still remembers how you treated it.

Ties of blood and office count for more. A family whose member sits on the throne, stands as heir, or married into your line warms to you more. So does a family whose cities enjoy your luxuries.
