About This Game

Forester: Reckoning is the Forester story lived rather than managed. You carry your father's ledger down to the harbour on the last ordinary evening, you hear the watch at the door that night, and you stand in the square at dawn. Then you run.

In the old woods north-east of the city there is a clearing, and in it a cabin someone burned twenty years ago. You fell the spruce and raise the walls again. You trade six logs for rye seed with a charcoal burner who asks too many questions. You hunt with his old bow, hide in the trees from the man with the lantern, and keep a fire alive through a blizzard night.

And then people start coming up the road. Raise them cabins, set them to work — felling, farming, baking, cutting stone — and rebuild what you have built in the style of its age: log, then timber and plaster as Hamburg builds, then red brick as the Hanse builds, and at last a city of stucco and street lamps.

Fourteen chapters of story lead you there, and then it is yours.

Game Features

  • A Story in First PersonFourteen chapters, from the harbour at Hamburg to a reckoning in the clearing. Your brother or sister is with you the whole way.
  • Stealth and FlightLanes, lanterns and the marsh gate. Crouch, lean round corners, keep out of the light.
  • The WoodsFell trees, split firewood, dig and sow, and hunt roe deer and hares with a bow — arrows that drop, and stick where they land.
  • SeasonsSpring, summer, autumn and a winter that burns a log a day in every hearth. Nothing grows; the stores are all you have.
  • A SettlementCabins, a bakery, a well, a quarry, a sawmill, a brickworks, a mine and smelter, a market, a church and a town hall — and people who go home at night.
  • From Clearing to CityRebuild every building in the style of the next age, until the square is cobbled, then paved and lit.
  • A Cabin That Is YoursWalk in, furnish it, bank the hearth, and sleep through the night in your own bed.
  • Made HereEvery model built in Blender, every sound and note of music synthesised — no stock assets.
