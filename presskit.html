DeadlyDog Productions is an indie game studio founded by Roan Fraese in 2025. It makes historical games about ordinary people caught up in large events, from a family driven out of Hamburg in 1683 to the nations of the Napoleonic age.

Two of its games tell the same story. Forester is a free pixel-art colony sim in the browser. Forester: Reckoning tells the same story in first person and in 3D, as a survival game, a city builder, a battle game and a grand strategy in one. Standards in Smoke is a free hex-map strategy game set in the age of muskets.

Hamburg, 1683. Your father is condemned for “malicious affairs” and his name struck from the rolls. You run through the marsh gate before the watch can take you too. Survive the woods, build a cabin into a town and the town into a city, stand in the line at Vienna, and rule a kingdom on the campaign map, all in first person, in 3D.
