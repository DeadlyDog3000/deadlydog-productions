# DeadlyDog Productions

The website of DeadlyDog Productions, an indie game studio founded by Roan Fraese in 2025.

**[deadlydog3000.github.io/deadlydog-productions](https://deadlydog3000.github.io/deadlydog-productions/)**

## The games

- **[Forester: Reckoning](https://deadlydog3000.github.io/deadlydog-productions/reckoning.html)**: Hamburg, 1683, in first
  person and 3D. A story, a survival game, a city builder, a battle game and a grand strategy, in one game. For Mac and
  Windows, with a free demo.
- **[Forester](https://deadlydog3000.github.io/deadlydog-productions/forester.html)**: a pixel-art colony survival game.
  Free in your browser.
- **[Standards In Smoke](https://deadlydog3000.github.io/deadlydog-productions/standards-in-smoke.html)**: a
  Napoleonic-era hex strategy game. Free in your browser.

Writing about the games? The [press kit](https://deadlydog3000.github.io/deadlydog-productions/presskit.html) has
facts, logos, screenshots and trailers.
